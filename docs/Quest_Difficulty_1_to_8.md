# Quest difficulty: 1?8 skulls

Skulls estimate the average level of a healthy three-person party over the whole expedition. They do not sum character levels or measure duration. Dispatch allows one to three members; smaller parties face greater risk.

- **1 skull:** safe for an unequipped level-one solo founder.
- **2?7 skulls:** estimated party level two through seven, with increasing defensive requirements and attrition.
- **8 skulls:** potentially deadly even at level eight. Strong equipment improves survival without guaranteeing it.

Schema 31 rebalances classes, powers, enemies and rewards together. There are 264 quests. Every combination of 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 or 30 minutes and one through eight skulls contains at least five quests. One- and three-minute catalogues are excluded from expansion. Existing in-flight expeditions preserve their previous combat model; earned ritual unlocks remain intact.
